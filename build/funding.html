VERIFICATION AND PAYMENT CONTROLS

Why Verification May Be Required

  • G2G applies identity verification and KYC/AML controls where required as part of its procedures for processing client transactions.
  • These requirements help determine how deposits and withdrawals can be processed and may affect the payment method, processing time, or additional information required for a transaction.

CLIENT FUNDS

How G2G Handles Client Funds

Review Security Of Funds Policy

G2G’s Security of Funds Policy provides for client funds to be held separately from the company’s operational funds. Key controls include:

  • Segregated client accounts
  • Daily reconciliation of client funds
  • Controlled access to financial systems
  • Dual authorization for fund transfers

These controls form part of G2G’s framework for the handling and oversight of client funds.
