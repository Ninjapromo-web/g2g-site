Index Trading Questions

Which index instruments can I trade with G2G?

G2G’s confirmed index offering includes US100, US500, US30, and DAX40. Supported futures and spot instruments are available across these US and European index markets.

What is the minimum index trade size?

The minimum trading volume is 0.01 lot. The maximum trading volume is 10 lots per trade, with a maximum of 50 net open positions per instrument.

What leverage is available for index trading?

Index leverage is dynamic, up to 1:500, subject to applicable terms and conditions.

Are index spreads fixed?

No. Index spreads are variable and may differ depending on market conditions and liquidity. The published index specification starts from 50 points.

When can I trade indices?

US100, US500, and US30 follow G2G’s US index trading schedule, while DAX40 has separate trading hours. All published trading hours are based on G2G server time (GMT+3) and may be affected by holiday schedules or market closures.
