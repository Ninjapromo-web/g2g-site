Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply to the IBs & Affiliates programme?

The programme is available to introducing brokers and affiliates interested in developing a business relationship with G2G.

What does the IBs & Affiliates programme include?

The programme includes multiple rebate types, a multi-tier IB structure, marketing support, and collaboration. Customizable offers are subject to Terms & Conditions.

Are Partnership terms fixed?

G2G does not present a fixed public tariff for Partnerships. Institutional and corporate requirements are discussed directly with the G2G team.

Who is Partnership intended for?

Partnerships are intended for institutional and corporate clients. This includes hedge funds, asset managers, institutions, and other corporate businesses.

What happens after I submit a Partnership enquiry?

The G2G team will review the information provided and respond to discuss the appropriate next steps.

Does submitting a Partnership enquiry open a trading account?

No. A Partnership enquiry is separate from G2G’s live trading account registration process.
