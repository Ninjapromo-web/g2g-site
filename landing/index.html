FREQUENTLY ASKED

What is G2G’s regulatory status?

G2G is regulated by the Saint Lucia Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSC). Before opening an account, please ensure that our services are available in your jurisdiction and review any local regulatory requirements that may apply.

How do withdrawals work?

Withdrawal requests are processed securely through your Client Portal using your available funding methods. Most requests are processed within 24 hours, subject to verification and payment provider processing times.

What funding methods are available?

You can fund your account using bank cards, bank transfers, cryptocurrencies including USDT, and supported e-wallets such as Neteller and Skrill. Available methods may vary depending on your country.

What if I have no trading experience?

A demo account lets you explore the platform and practice trading strategies before opening a live account.

What account is available?

G2G offers one retail account — the Individual Account. It gives you access to all available markets on MT5 from a $10 minimum deposit. A swap-free (Islamic) option is available for eligible clients.
